§1 — the furniture: one decision, two surfaces

Everything on screen in this product is drawn in a hand-made, wobbled-ink style. Two things are not, and they are the two things on screen the most. Deciding them separately produces two answers that do not match, so this page answers them once: the lower third is drawn in the kit's hand; captions get the kit's materials and not its hand. Both below, rendered together, over the grounds they actually have to survive.

The principle that splits them is scale and duration, not taste. A wobbled edge is a mark at 78px and a rendering fault at 22px. The lower third is large, persistent and a brand element, so the hand reads as craft. A caption is small, changes every two seconds, and libass re-renders it per frame with no boil index — so the kit's hand there would either sit dead still (and look like a failed effect) or re-wobble every frame (and crawl). Same kit, two registers, one reason.
