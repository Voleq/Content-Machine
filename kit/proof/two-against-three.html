Two frames against three

The same plate, boiled both ways, playing side by side. §1.1 asks for this to be judged in motion rather than asserted, so the decision is not “2 or 3” but “2 or 3 at what rate” — hence the slider. Three plates, because they do not behave alike: a room moves 22% of its ink and a head 5.4%, and one sample cannot answer a question being asked of all ninety-six.

What to watch for: on the two-frame side the eye finds the alternation and locks onto it, and from that moment the plate is vibrating between two known states rather than living. The lock takes about a second at 2fps and arrives faster the slower you go.
