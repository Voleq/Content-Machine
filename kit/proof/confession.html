§2 — the confession: two treatments

Roughly one video in three carries the moment the host says he got something wrong about this company before. The pipeline tracks them in a ledger; on screen it has been reaching for whatever generic plate the chapter happened to have. Two treatments below, filled with real words, side by side in both aspects — my recommendation is ledger, and the reasoning is under the renders.

What both plates refuse, in the geometry rather than in a comment. No cross, no strike-through, no red, no rule through the old claim. All three bodies are one type role at one size in structure, so neither plate can mark which of them is the bad one. The register is a person saying plainly "I had this wrong, here is what I missed" — a credibility move, not an apology — and a plate that performs either the shame or the shrug has taken the tone out of the host's hands.

And it is not said-happened re-pointed. That plate is a company's claim against the outcome, drawn as a two-track timeline and built to read as an indictment. Aimed at the host it would make the plate an indictment of him, which is the opposite of what a confession is for.
