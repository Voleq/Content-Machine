paper — headline bands
======================

USE WHEN
  A section opens, or a claim needs stating in words before it is shown in
  numbers. This is the family that carries titles; nothing else does.

MEMBERS
  headline-band-t1   the plain band. Default.
  headline-band-t2   heavier treatment. For a section opener.
  headline-band-t3   the loudest. Once per video — the thesis.

  All three in both aspects.

SLOTS
  kicker     section or segment name. Short, above.
  headline   the claim. One sentence, no full stop. The maxChars limit is
             real: past it, the line hits the rule below.
  sub        the qualifier or the source. Optional on t1, usually populated
             on t2 and t3.

SEQUENCE
  1  kicker
  2  headline, on its own beat, and hold — this is a reading frame
  3  sub

  A band composited over a room plate wants wide-tight or desk-front; the
  full wide angle has no clear top third.

TRAPS
  * Escalating treatments across a video (t1, t2, t3) reads as a build. Do
    not use t3 early and then step down.
  * A headline is not a sentence from the voiceover. If the reader can read
    it while hearing the same words, one of the two is redundant.
