overlays — row-band
===================

USE WHEN
  A row in a table or a step in a ladder is being spoken about. This is the
  only sanctioned way to emphasise a row.

MEMBER
  row-band   1744 x 112 canvas, one slot: area. Second ground hatch, bracketed
             top and bottom in structure. The rules are not decoration: the
             numbers sheet zebra-stripes its even rows in ground2 already, so a
             ground2 band alone is invisible on half the sheet.

SLOTS
  area   the band's own extent. Composite into the target's band-N slot,
         stretched in x ONLY: keep the band's native 112-unit height and centre
         it in the row. Stretched in y as well, the hatch degenerates into a
         solid slab and the row reads as filled in rather than pointed at.

SEQUENCE
  Lit on the beat the voiceover reaches the row. Off before the next row
  lights. One at a time, without exception — a table with three bands lit has
  emphasised nothing.

TRAPS
  * The band is second ground (#CBBC9B) and carries no meaning. It says
    "here", not "good" or "bad". Direction stays in the cells, and attention
    belongs to annotations. (It was drawn in attention until the compositor put
    a band and a scrawl in the same frame and the frame had two attentions.)
  * Do not tint cell backgrounds as an alternative. The band exists so that
    emphasis and meaning never use the same channel.
  * Valid targets: any band-N in tables/*, structure/unit-ladder. Nothing
    else has a band slot, and nothing else should be given one.
