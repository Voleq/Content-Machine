shorts — hook cards
===================

USE WHEN
  The first two seconds of a vertical cut. These exist to stop a scroll and
  nothing else. 9:16 only; there is no landscape hook.

MEMBERS
  hook-card-t1   ticker, move, hook, sub. The standard: symbol and the move
                 that justifies the video.
  hook-card-t2   same slots, different weight. Alternate so a channel's
                 cards do not all look identical.
  hook-card-t3   ticker, huge, hook, sub. huge replaces move — for a single
                 dominant number rather than a percentage change.

SLOTS
  ticker   the symbol. Always populated.
  move     the change, with sign. Takes up or down — the only slot in the
           family that carries direction.
  huge     (t3) the number itself, largest element on the plate.
  hook     the question or claim. Under ten words; a hook that needs reading
           has already failed.
  sub      the qualifier — period, basis, or the "despite".

SEQUENCE
  All four slots land on frame one. There is no reveal on a hook card; a
  build costs the attention it was made to capture. Hold two seconds, then
  cut to the room.

TRAPS
  * move without a sign is not a move.
  * The hook must be answered in the video. A hook card whose question the
    script does not resolve is the one thing that loses a returning viewer.
  * Do not put the host on a hook card. He arrives on the cut after.
