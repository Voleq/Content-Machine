charts — the shape
==================

USE WHEN
  The reader already accepts the numbers and now needs their shape. Always
  after a table of the same series, never before.

MEMBERS
  line-6y      six periods, points labelled. The default series chart.
  bars-6y      six periods, bars. Use when the periods are independent
               quantities rather than a path — a bar invites comparison
               between periods, a line asserts continuity.
  line-dense   many observations, four period heads, three marks. Use for a
               price or a daily series where individual points are not the
               point.

  All three in both aspects.

SLOTS
  unit         units line
  plot-area    the drawing area. No text. Use it to place a host so he never
               overlaps the plot, and to bound any annotation.
  y-1..y-5     y-axis labels, bottom to top (line-dense has four)
  head-1..6    period labels, oldest left
  point-N / bar-N    the mark's own position, per period. This is where a
               value marker, dot or cap goes — not the number.
  value-N      the number for period N, positioned clear of the mark.
  line-dense instead of point/value:
    mark-high  the peak. Label it with the value and the date.
    mark-low   the trough.
    mark-last  the latest observation. Always populated; it is the "and now"
               of the sentence.

SEQUENCE
  1  unit
  2  y labels, then head labels — the frame before the data
  3  points or bars, oldest to newest, one period per beat if the voiceover
     is walking the series; all at once if it is not
  4  value-N only for the periods being spoken about. Six labelled values is
     a table with extra steps.
  For line-dense: the series, then mark-last, then whichever of
  mark-high / mark-low the argument needs. Rarely both.

TRAPS
  * dataPolicy in the manifest: the plate draws the axis furniture and the
    plot area, not the series. The series is composited into plot-area. Do
    not expect a chart plate to know its own data.
  * The subject's own series is structure (#242A31), not neutral data. Peers
    and consensus are the other party (#6A5C8B). If the subject's line is
    coloured like everyone else's, the frame has no subject.
  * A single period in attention. Not the whole series.
