figures — one number, large
==========================

USE WHEN
  A single quantity is the beat. The frame's job is to make it unforgettable
  and then get out of the way. If two numbers matter, use compare-*; if
  three do, it is a table.

MEMBERS
  big-number-l1     kicker, value, label, caption. One line of label.
  big-number-l2     adds detail — a second label line, for a qualifier the
                    number cannot carry alone ("...at the midpoint of guidance").
  big-fraction      numerator over denominator, both labelled, with a result.
                    Use when the number is a ratio and the reader should see
                    what it is made of.
  compare-side      two quantities, side by side. 16:9 has detail-1/detail-2;
                    9:16 replaces them with a single delta.
  compare-stacked   two quantities, one above the other, with a delta. Use
                    when the two are the same measure at two times; side-by-
                    side is for two different things at the same time.

SLOTS
  kicker      what this is. Small, above. Sets the frame, never repeats the
              label.
  value       the number. Largest thing on the plate.
  label       what it measures.
  detail      the qualifier (l2, compare-side 16:9 only)
  caption     source or basis. Lowest, smallest. Always populated on any
              figure that came from a filing.
  big-fraction: numerator / numerator-label / denominator / denominator-label
              / result
  compare-*:  label-1 / value-1 / label-2 / value-2, plus delta or detail-N

SEQUENCE
  1  kicker
  2  value — the whole point, do not build up to it
  3  label
  4  detail, if present
  5  caption
  compare-*: kicker, then side 1 complete, then side 2 complete, then delta.
  Never both values at once — the frame is an argument about the second one.

TRAPS
  * The value takes a colour only if it has direction. A revenue figure is
    neutral data even in a segment about decline.
  * delta takes up or down and gets the sign. If it needs a sentence to be
    understood, it belongs in caption.
  * kicker is not a title. Titles go on paper/headline-band.
