cards — the aside
=================

USE WHEN
  The narration has to stop and define, quote, or test something before it
  can continue. A card is a deliberate interruption; the host does not
  appear on one.

MEMBERS
  definition   kicker, term, body, example. For a term the reader cannot be
               assumed to know. The example slot is not optional — a
               definition without one has not been made concrete.
  quote-pull   body, attribution, source. For management's own words. Use
               verbatim; if it needs an ellipsis, the quote is too long for
               the frame.
  criteria     title, three check/row pairs, caption. A test being applied.
               check-N takes a mark, row-N the condition.

SLOTS
  definition   kicker / term / body / example
  quote-pull   body / attribution / source
  criteria     title / check-1 / row-1 / check-2 / row-2 / check-3 / row-3 /
               caption

SEQUENCE
  definition   kicker, term, body, then example on its own beat.
  quote-pull   body first and whole — the reader reads before they are told
               who said it. Then attribution, then source.
  criteria     title, then row-N and its check-N together, one pair per beat.
               Never all three checks at once; the suspense of each check is
               the frame's only movement.

TRAPS
  * criteria checks take up or down. Three ups is not a test, it is a claim.
  * quote-pull's source is the filing or call, with a date. "Company" is not
    a source.
  * Cards do not stack. Two definitions in a row means the script needs
    rewriting, not a second card.
