frames — for other people's material
====================================

USE WHEN
  Something not drawn by this engine has to appear on screen: a filing
  excerpt, a screenshot, a photograph, a chart from a source.

MEMBERS
  media-frame-t1/t2/t3   a bordered area with caption and source. Three
                         weights; pick by how much the frame should compete
                         with what is in it. t1 for a busy image, t3 for a
                         sparse one.
  capture-frame          for text captures. Has headline, body and a mark —
                         the mark is what you are pointing at inside the
                         capture.

SLOTS
  media-frame     media (the area the asset drops into — nothing drawn) /
                  caption / source
  capture-frame   source / headline / body / mark / caption

SEQUENCE
  media-frame   frame up, media in, caption, then source. Source is never
                omitted.
  capture-frame source, headline, body, then mark last — the mark is the
                point of the frame and lands after the reader has read.

TRAPS
  * media is an area, not a slot to draw in. Fit the asset to it; do not
    scale the frame to the asset.
  * The frame does not crop. If the source material is the wrong aspect,
    letterbox it inside media rather than filling.
  * capture-frame's body is an excerpt, not a summary. Summaries go in
    caption.
