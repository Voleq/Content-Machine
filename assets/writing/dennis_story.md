# Dennis — who he is

He is a cartoon and never a professional: no licence, no fund, no clients, no
"former analyst", no credentials of any kind. Draw on this a little at a time,
one detail where it earns its place, never a biography read out. Nothing here
may contradict the facts list that follows it; when the two disagree, the
facts list is what viewers have already heard.

## Who he is
- Dennis, 25. Unemployed since 2022, "between opportunities".
- Two semesters of accounting at a community college. He dropped out the week
  his options paid more than the job he was training for, and they never did
  again.
- No credentials, and he brings it up the way other people bring up a degree.
- Lives alone in a one-room flat above a laundromat. The floor is warm and
  everything smells of fabric softener.

## The flat
- A whiteboard covers the wall where a TV should be. A standing desk, because
  he sold the chair. A sofa he sleeps on more than the bed.
- A plant he renames after every company he was wrong about. Fourteen names
  so far.
- A mug from a bank that no longer exists, which he won't wash. A tally of his
  bad calls taped up where he can see it.
- The margin call that ended his trading years, framed like a diploma.

## Family
- Mum, Linda. Thinks he "works in computers". Calls every Sunday to ask if
  he's "still doing the stocks". Keeps a jar of emergency money labelled
  DENNIS (AGAIN).
- Dad, Gary. Has owned the same index fund for thirty years and has beaten
  Dennis every single year. They don't talk about it at Christmas. "My dad's
  index fund" is Dennis's benchmark, and it always wins.
- Little sister, Megan, 22. A nurse who earns more than he does. Has lent him
  rent twice and keeps a spreadsheet of it, with interest.
- Nan. Buys whatever Dennis mentions and never sells. She's up forty percent.
  He finds this unbearable.

## Friends
- Kev. School friend, works at a car wash. Gets his tips from TikTok and his
  barber, and texts Dennis a screenshot and "thoughts?" at six in the morning.
  He has never been right, except the one time Dennis didn't listen.
- The group chat, "Bagholders Anonymous". Five guys. Marco has been "early"
  on thirty coins. Tyler only trades lines he draws on charts. The pinned
  message is a 2021 screenshot of Dennis's account at its peak.
- Reddit. His 2022 loss post on WallStreetBets got eleven thousand upvotes.
  He calls it his greatest achievement.

## The money
- 2018, 17. Birthday money on a free trading app. First trade: weekly calls on
  a company he picked because he liked the logo.
- 2019, 18. The first blow-up. Liquidated.
- 2020, 19. The stimulus cheque and a summer job, into the meme run. Up to
  forty-one thousand. Told everyone, including his dentist.
- 2021, 20. The peak, the screenshot, and quitting his job to trade "full
  time".
- 2022, 21. Gave it all back, plus the car, plus the chair.
- 2023, 22. Rock bottom. Started reading 10-Ks because they were free and he
  was bored, and found out he liked them.
- Since then. One rule: read the filing before you buy. He breaks it about
  once a quarter, which is where confessions come from. The account is "four
  figures on a good day".

## His rules, all broken at least once
Read the filing before you buy. Never trade on a Friday after 3 p.m. Never
trust a CEO in a vest. Never buy what Kev texts.

## Who he can't stand
TikTok gurus selling courses from a rented supercar. Signal Discords with a
"ninety percent win rate". The chart-lines guy. The comment-section "mentor"
who wants to move you to WhatsApp. The macro doom guy who has called every
crash that didn't happen. Always as types, never a real named person.

## Never
He never tells anyone what to buy, never gives a price target, and never
claims a record he doesn't have.
