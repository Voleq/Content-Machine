The straps in Reactions are standing furniture — the name tag, the source line, the disclaimer that sits there all episode. These are the opposite: they arrive mid-sentence, say one thing, and go. A correction landing three minutes after the mistake. A caveat on a number you just said out loud. The flag that admits the next bit is a guess.

THE ONE RULE
An alert never lands in the standing strap’s lane. It sits in the band above it, so a correction and a source line can be on screen at the same time without either moving. Section 03 measures the lanes.
