A recap board is not an agenda — the agenda promises, the recap accounts. It runs at the end of a chapter and shows what the last eight minutes actually established, what got ruled out, and what is still open. The board accumulates across the episode: the same object, three states, so the viewer sees the argument being built rather than being told it was.

THE RULE THAT MAKES IT WORK
A line only moves to settled if a number on screen put it there. Anything that came from tone, management’s framing, or a hunch stays in open — even when everyone in the room believes it. That constraint is the whole reason the board is worth watching.
