Example Corp Announces AI Partnership with a Cloud Provider

SPRINGFIELD, July 6, 2026

SPRINGFIELD, July 6, 2026 -- Example Corp (NASDAQ: EXMPL), a provider of logistics routing software, today announced a strategic partnership to bring generative AI features to its routing platform. The companies will jointly develop route-planning assistants for mid-sized carriers. Financial terms were not disclosed, and the agreement includes no minimum purchase commitment.

"This partnership accelerates our AI roadmap," said the chief executive of Example Corp.
