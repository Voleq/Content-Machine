Item 1. Business

Example Corp designs and sells a cloud-based logistics-software platform to mid-market carriers and shippers. We generate revenue primarily through recurring subscriptions and usage-based fees. Our platform coordinates freight routing, capacity matching, and settlement across a network of partners.

We operate in a highly competitive market and compete with both established enterprise vendors and a growing number of well-funded startups. Our long-term success depends on continued adoption of our platform and on our ability to expand within our existing customer base.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Investing in our common stock involves a high degree of risk. You should carefully consider the risks described below before making an investment decision.

We have incurred net losses in each year since inception, and we may never achieve or sustain profitability. Our net losses were $89.0 million, $70.0 million, and $49.0 million for the years ended December 31, 2025, 2024, and 2023, respectively. We expect our operating expenses to increase as we continue to invest in our platform and go-to-market functions.

Stock-based compensation expense has grown to represent a significant and increasing portion of our total operating expenses. Continued issuances dilute the ownership interests of existing stockholders, and we expect stock-based compensation to remain a material expense for the foreseeable future.

We have a substantial amount of indebtedness, and our leverage could adversely affect our business and our ability to meet our obligations. If we are unable to generate sufficient cash flow, we may be required to refinance on unfavorable terms or curtail investment in the business.
