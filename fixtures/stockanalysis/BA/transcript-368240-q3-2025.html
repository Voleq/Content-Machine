Earnings Call: Q3 2025

Oct 29, 2025

  • Quarterly report
  • Slides
  • Earnings release

Summary

Revenue grew 30% to $23.3B with positive free cash flow, but a $4.9B 777X charge led to a core loss. Commercial and defense segments saw strong demand and record backlogs, while production rates for 737 and 787 are rising. 777X delivery delayed to 2027, with cash neutrality expected by 2028.
